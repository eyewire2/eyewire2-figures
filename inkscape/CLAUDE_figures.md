These are instructions for updating the figures from the panels

The figures are in the folder `inkscape` and in `.svg` format; they must be compatible to InkScape.
The figure panels are generated by iPython scripts.
The current version of the panels are in `eyewire2-figures-dev` and there in the subfolders called `v2026-09-16-15h`

In all figures, replace all-caps ON and OFF by On and Off, respectively

Bipolar cell type names need to be checked in every files:

Old	-> New
t1 -> t1a
GluMI -> t1b/GluMI
t5o	-> t5a/o
t5t	-> t5b/t
t5i	-> t5c/i
XBC	-> t5d/XBC
